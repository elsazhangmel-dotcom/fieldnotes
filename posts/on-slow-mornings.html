On slow mornings

July 14, 2026 · 2 minute read

The first hour of the day decides more than it should. If it starts in other people’s urgency, the rest of the day tends to follow.

A slow morning does not need to be luxurious. It can be coffee, a window, and ten minutes without a screen. Enough space to notice what you actually want to do.

I have started treating that hour as a small room I refuse to rent out. Messages can wait. News can wait. Even good ideas can wait until I have said hello to myself first.

Protect the quiet, and the quiet will protect your attention.

This is not a productivity trick. It is a boundary. The day will still be full — but it does not have to begin already crowded.
