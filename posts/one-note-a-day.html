One note a day

July 5, 2026 · 1 minute read

For thirty days I wrote one short note every evening — no editing pass, no audience in mind, five minutes maximum.

The goal was not a better archive. It was a better noticing muscle. Most days the note was ordinary. A few days it caught something I would have scrolled past.

What I learned

Consistency beat quality. The nights I felt least inspired still produced useful breadcrumbs. The nights I tried to be clever produced less.

I am keeping the habit, but loosely: missing a day is fine. Forcing a performance is not.
